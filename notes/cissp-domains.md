# 🛡️ CISSP Security Domains - Study Notes

These are my notes based on the eight CISSP (Certified Information Systems Security Professional) security domains, which help organize the work of cybersecurity professionals. These were covered in the Google Cybersecurity Certificate course.

---

## 1. Security and Risk Management
- The focus of the security and risk management domain is defining security goals and objectives, risk mitigation, compliance, business continuity, and regulations.
- Key concepts: InfoSec, incident response, business continuity, GDPR, and security posture
- Tools: Playbooks, training programs

## 2. Asset Security
- Focus: Protecting organizational assets (data and hardware)
- Key actions: Data backups, impact analysis, recovery planning
- Goal: Minimize exposure and track asset lifecycles

## 3. Security Architecture and Engineering
- Focus: Data protection through secure design
- Key concepts: Least privilege, defense in depth, zero trust
- Shared responsibility between engineers and analysts

## 4. Communication and Network Security
- Focus: Secure communication (wired, wireless, cloud)
- Covers: On-site, remote, and hybrid network protections
- Controls: Restricted access, firewalls, encryption

## 5. Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- Focus: Who has access to what, and why
- Principle: Least privilege and authentication
- Tools: Role-based access, MFA, SSO

## 6. Security Assessment and Testing
- Focus: Finding vulnerabilities before attackers do
- Key practices: Penetration testing, auditing permissions, and control testing

## 7. Security Operations
- Focus: Responding to breaches and preparing for future attacks
- Tools: SIEM, IDS/IPS, incident documentation
- Practices: Forensics, playbooks, log management

## 8. Software Development Security
- Focus: Building secure apps from the ground up
- Involves: Secure coding practices and vulnerability testing
- Goal: Keep users and data safe through the entire dev lifecycle
