
# 🛡️ Risk & Vulnerability Awareness

As a cybersecurity student and aspiring analyst, I actively study industry frameworks, threat models, and vulnerability databases to strengthen my understanding of digital risk.

## 🔍 Risk Management Strategies

Organizations protect digital and physical assets using these strategies:
- **Acceptance**: Accept the risk to avoid disrupting business.
- **Avoidance**: Eliminate risk with proactive changes.
- **Transference**: Shift the risk to a third party.
- **Mitigation**: Reduce the potential impact of a risk.

I follow frameworks like:
- [NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-37/rev-2/final)
- [HITRUST CSF](https://hitrustalliance.net)

## 🚨 Common Threats

- **Insider Threats**: Staff misusing access.
- **Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)**: Long-term unauthorized access by external actors.

## ⚠️ Common Risks

- **External/Internal Risks**
- **Legacy Systems**
- **Multiparty Vendor Exposure**
- **Outdated Software Licensing & Patching**

Resources:
- [NIST Cybersecurity Risks List](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)
- [OWASP Top Ten](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/)

## 🧱 Vulnerability Management

- I research and monitor high-profile vulnerabilities:
  - **ProxyLogon** (Microsoft Exchange)
  - **ZeroLogon** (Netlogon)
  - **Log4Shell** (Java Logging Library)
  - **PetitPotam** (Windows NTLM)
  - **Server-side request forgery (SSRF)**

Databases I follow:
- [NIST National Vulnerability Database](https://nvd.nist.gov)
- [CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

Mitigation includes identifying vulnerabilities, applying patches, and configuring monitoring systems to detect potential exploits early.
